How interest is calculated

FundNiti uses simple interest on the original principal. Interest is not compounded, and repayments do not reduce the interest calculation base.

Monthly interest = Principal × Monthly rate ÷ 100

For a partial monthly anniversary period, the app prorates the monthly interest by elapsed days divided by the actual number of days in that period.

Example: ₹10,000 at 2% per month earns ₹200 for a full monthly period. For 10 days of a 30-day period, the prorated amount is ₹66.67. Confirm the applicable method against your agreement before settlement.

Backups & recovery

Where is the automatic backup stored?

Download/FundNiti/Backup/Lendora-Data.json

Is the automatic JSON backup encrypted?

No. The automatic snapshot is unencrypted and each backup replaces the previous snapshot with the latest full database state. It contains sensitive financial records; protect your phone and do not share the file.

Can encrypted manual backups move to another phone?

Manual .lendora exports are encrypted using Android Keystore and are generally tied to the original installation/device key. Keep an additional backup copy and verify your recovery needs before changing devices.

What happens when upgrading from the older app ID?

com.fundniti installs separately from the older com.rath0darya.loanmanager app. Keep its portable Lendora-Data.json backup and restore it in FundNiti; encrypted exports remain tied to their original installation key.
